A useful student laptop is the one that lasts a class day, opens the sites school actually uses, and can be repaired when a key dies. Weight matters more than a gaming graphic chip.

What we look at on the bench:

- Battery health, not just the sticker claim
- Keyboard and hinge play
- RAM you can live with for a few years
- A charger that is included
