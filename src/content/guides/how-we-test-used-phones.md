Before a phone is listed it is charged, signed out of the last owner’s accounts, and checked like a person would use it on day one.

- Screen for spots and a true touch
- Front and back cameras
- Speaker, earpiece, and microphone
- Buttons, charge port, and Wi-Fi
- Battery that holds a normal day, described honestly if it does not
